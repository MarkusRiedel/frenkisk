#### ⚠⚠⚠ THE FEMININE IS CITED IN THE ACCUSATIVE, NOT IN THE NOMINATIVE

**The 203 feminines marked `(f.)` with no bracket are cited here in `-a`. Their NOMINATIVE is in `-u`.**

| | form |
|---|---|
| what the entry writes | **saka** |
| nominative sg. (subject, **predicate**) | **saku** |
| accusative sg. (object) | saka |
| genitive sg. | saka |
| dative sg. | **saku** |

⚠⚠ **If you copy the lemma as it stands for a SUBJECT or a PREDICATE, you are writing an accusative.** *“That is no reason” wants `nihain saku`, not †`nihain saka`.*

⚠ **It is the only class in this case.** `(m.) dag`, `(n.) word`, `[n] hano` all cite their nominative ; the feminine in `-ō` is the exception, and it is the third class of the lexicon by numbers. Other examples: `geba` / **gebu**, `wahsunga` / **wahsungu**.

⚠ The grammar said it already, in its table of classes. *A translator looking for a word goes to the index, not to the table of classes — a rule filed where nobody goes protects as much as a rule that is absent.* Carried here on 2026-08-26, after the 66th round.

#### ⚠⚠ THE PRESENT OF THE STRONG VERBS RAISES ITS VOWEL

**The dictionary cites the INFINITIVE. In the singular of the present, the vowel of the root is raised ; in the plural it does not move.**

| class | infinitive | **singular** | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| **II** | `beudan` | **biudu, biudis, biudid** | beudam, beudid, beudand |
| **III, IV, V** | `geban` | **gibu, gibis, gibid** | gebam, gebid, geband |

• class **II**: `eu` → **`iu`** — *keusan* → **kiusid**, *sleutan* → **sliutid**, *fleugan* → **fliugid**
• classes **III, IV, V**: `e` → **`i`** — *helpan* → **hilpid**, *beran* → **birid**, *lesan* → **lisid**

⚠⚠ **All THREE persons of the singular raise, the 1st included** — `gibu`, not †`gebu`: the `-u` of the ending assimilates just as the `-i-` does. **No person of the plural raises**, not even the 2nd `gebid`, whose `i` is in the ending and not in the root.

⚠⚠⚠ **60 strong verbs are concerned, and 43 write this form nowhere** — neither here nor in the register of principal parts, which does not carry the present. **Derive it by the rule above.** *The project deliberately refuses to manufacture the forms no witness writes: the vowel serves it as a filter, never as a mould.*

⚠ The complete rule and its bounds: **law L4** in the grammar. Carried here on 2026-08-26.
